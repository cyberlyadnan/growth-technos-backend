We build custom software for a living, so you might expect us to say "always go custom". We won't. For many Indian brands, Shopify or WooCommerce is the right place to start.

The real question is when a ready-made platform stops being enough. This guide helps you decide.

## Shopify: fast and easy, with ongoing costs

Shopify gets you live quickly, with good themes, an app for almost everything and no server to manage.

What to keep in mind:
- You pay a monthly subscription fee in USD or INR.
- Most useful features come as separate apps, each with its own monthly charge that compounds.
- Depending on how you set up payments, there may be extra platform transaction fees on top of your payment gateway charges.
- Custom business logic, such as complex wholesale dealer pricing, is limited to what third-party apps allow.

Check Shopify's current pricing and payment rules for India before deciding, because they change.

## WooCommerce: flexible, with more upkeep

WooCommerce runs on WordPress. It is flexible, has thousands of plugins and no platform license fee.

What to keep in mind:
- You need high-speed cloud hosting and a developer to maintain it.
- Plugins need regular updates, and they sometimes conflict with each other after core WordPress updates.
- Performance and page speed require active attention as products and visitor traffic grow.

We built the Zakaria Perfumes store on WooCommerce, and for a focused product catalogue with cart, checkout, and Indian UPI payments, it works very well.

## Custom e-commerce: when it is worth it

A custom-built store built on Next.js and Node.js makes sense when your business doesn't fit a standard retail template:
- **B2B and B2C on one site:** Wholesale prices for verified dealers and retail prices for public shoppers.
- **Product configurators:** Printing, custom packaging, apparel or furniture where price depends dynamically on material, size, and quantity.
- **Multi-vendor marketplaces:** Where other sellers list products and receive split payouts. Review our [multi-vendor marketplace development cost guide](/blog/multi-vendor-marketplace-development-cost-india).
- **Deep ERP & Tally integration:** Orders, stock and GST invoices stay in continuous sync. Learn about [Tally integration with custom software](/blog/tally-integration-custom-software).
- **High order volumes:** Where monthly Shopify app fees and transaction penalties add up to more than a custom build.

| Time to launch | Fastest | Fast | Slower (4-8 weeks) |
| Upfront cost | Low | Low to medium | Higher |
| Monthly costs | Plan + app fees | Hosting + maintenance | Hosting + maintenance |
| Custom business logic | Limited | Medium | 100% Full Control |
| Integration with your systems | Through apps | Through plugins or custom code | Built directly into DB |

## Indian details that matter on any platform

1. **Payment gateways:** Smooth integration with Razorpay, Cashfree, or PhonePe, with UPI QR and intent working without friction.
2. **Cash on Delivery (COD) rules:** OTP verification or WhatsApp confirmation to prevent fake orders and RTO losses.
3. **Logistics APIs:** Direct courier integration with Shiprocket or Delhivery for automated airway bill (AWB) generation.
4. **GST compliance:** Tax invoices with HSN codes generated correctly for every transaction.
5. **WhatsApp automation:** Order confirmation, dispatch updates and delivery tracking delivered on WhatsApp.

## A simple decision guide

- **New brand, standard products, testing the market:** Start with Shopify or WooCommerce.
- **Wholesale and retail together, configurable products or multiple vendors:** Choose custom development.
- **Already selling well, but paying heavily in recurring app fees:** Custom development is worth pricing.

### FAQ

**Is Shopify good for small businesses in India?**
Yes, for many. It is fast to launch and easy to run. Watch the monthly app costs and payment fees as you grow.

**Is WooCommerce cheaper than Shopify?**
There is no platform fee, but you pay for hosting, plugins and maintenance. The total depends on your setup.

**When should I build a custom e-commerce website?**
When you need wholesale and retail pricing together, configurable products, multiple vendors or deep integration with your inventory and accounts.

**Can my online store sync with Tally?**
Yes. Orders and invoices can sync with Tally through integration.
