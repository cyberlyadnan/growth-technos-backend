At the end of every month, someone in your office exports attendance from the biometric machine, cleans it in Excel, adds leaves from a register, works out overtime, applies PF and ESI, and calculates salaries. Then three employees dispute their pay.

It usually takes days, and mistakes are common. Attendance and payroll software turns those days into hours.

## Common problems with manual attendance and payroll

- Biometric data needs manual cleaning every month before it can be used.
- Overtime disputes because nobody agrees on the hours worked.
- Leave tracking in paper registers, with balances nobody trusts.
- Field staff and multiple project sites can't use the office biometric machine.
- Salary calculation errors from manual formulas, especially with complex deductions.
- Advances and salary loans tracked separately and forgotten at salary time. See how this connects to [production tracking](/blog/production-tracking-software-small-manufacturers).

## What attendance software should handle

**Several ways to mark attendance.** Biometric machine integration for the office or factory gate, a mobile app with geofenced location limits for field staff, face recognition, or QR scanning at sites.

**Shifts and overtime.** Different shifts, night shifts, rotational weekly offs and overtime formulas applied automatically.

**Leave management.** Leave applications on the phone, approvals by managers and live leave balances.

**Late marks and short hours,** calculated by your transparent rules, not by someone's subjective judgement each month.

## What payroll software should handle

**Salary structure per employee.** Basic pay, HRA, special allowances, production incentives and deductions.

**Statutory deductions.** PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS, where they apply.

**Advances and loans,** deducted automatically in agreed monthly instalments.

**Payslips generated and shared** automatically on WhatsApp or email.

**Reports for your CA.** Salary registers and statutory contribution summaries in the formats your CA needs. Read our [software maintenance AMC cost guide](/blog/software-maintenance-amc-cost-india).

## A note on compliance

Labour rules and deduction rates change from time to time. Good payroll software lets you update rates and rules without changing the underlying code. Always have your CA confirm your setup, especially when rules change.

## Ready-made or custom?

Ready-made HR and payroll apps work well for corporate offices with standard salaries and a single fixed shift.

Custom makes sense for factories, construction contractors and multi-site businesses with multiple shifts, contract workers, piece-rate wages, site-wise attendance, or when you want attendance and payroll connected to your ERP or production data. ### FAQ

**Can the software work with our existing biometric machine?**
Usually yes. Most biometric machines can export or sync data, which the software then processes automatically.

**Can field staff mark attendance from their phones?**
Yes, through a mobile app with location limits or face recognition, so attendance is marked only at the right place.

**Does payroll software calculate PF and ESI?**
Yes. Deductions are calculated from rates you set, and the rates can be updated when rules change. Have your CA confirm the setup.

**Can employees see their payslips and leave balance?**
Yes. Employees can check payslips, leave balances and apply for leave from their phones.
