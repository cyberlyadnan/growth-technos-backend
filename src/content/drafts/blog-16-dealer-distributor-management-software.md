If you sell through dealers, you probably know this routine. Orders arrive on WhatsApp, on phone calls, through salesmen and sometimes on a scrap of paper. Someone in the office types them up, checks the rate for that dealer, checks the outstanding, and hopes nothing was missed.

It works when you have 15 dealers. At 60 or 200, it starts to break.

## Where dealer businesses lose money

- **Missed orders:** A message sent late at night gets buried. The dealer orders from your competitor next time.
- **Wrong rates:** Every dealer has a different price list, discount or scheme. One wrong invoice means a dispute and a credit note.
- **Credit overrun:** Orders get dispatched to dealers who are already past their credit limit.
- **Scheme confusion:** "Buy 10 cartons, get 1 free" applied to some dealers and forgotten for others.
- **No view of secondary sales:** You know what you sold to the distributor, not what the distributor sold to retailers.

## What a good dealer management system does

**Dealer ordering app or portal.** Each dealer logs in on their phone and sees only their own price list, schemes and order history. They place orders any time, and nothing gets lost.

**Automatic pricing and schemes.** Dealer-wise rates, slab discounts and schemes apply on their own. Nobody has to remember them.

**Credit check before confirmation.** The system shows the outstanding and the credit limit. Orders over the limit go to the owner for approval.

**Stock check.** Orders are checked against available stock, with partial dispatch if needed.

**Order approval and dispatch.** Office staff confirm, pack and dispatch, and the dealer gets a WhatsApp update with transport details.

**Payments and outstanding.** Payment entries, ageing reports, and automatic reminders for overdue amounts.

**Salesman module.** Salesmen take orders on behalf of dealers, record visits and see their targets. Discover how our [field sales tracking app](/blog/field-sales-tracking-app-india) pairs with distribution networks.

**Secondary sales.** For brands that sell through distributors, distributors record their sales to retailers, so you finally see what is moving in the market.

**Tally sync.** Invoices flow into Tally, so your accountant keeps working the way they already do. Learn more about [Tally integration with custom software](/blog/tally-integration-custom-software).

## Rolling it out to dealers

The software is the easy part. Getting dealers to use it takes a plan:
1. Start with your top 20 dealers. They order most often and benefit most.
2. Send a short WhatsApp video showing how to place an order in under a minute.
3. Keep phone orders open at first, with office staff entering them into the system, so nobody feels forced.
4. Give a reason to switch, such as faster dispatch for app orders or a small scheme for ordering through the app.

### FAQ

**Can dealers place orders from their phones?**
Yes. Dealers get a simple app or mobile-friendly portal showing their own prices, schemes and order history.

**Can the system stop orders when a dealer crosses the credit limit?**
Yes. Orders over the limit can be blocked or sent to the owner for approval.

**Will it work with Tally?**
Yes. Invoices and payments can sync with Tally so accounts stay in one place.

**What is secondary sales tracking?**
It records what your distributors sell to retailers, so you can see actual market movement, not just your own sales to distributors.
