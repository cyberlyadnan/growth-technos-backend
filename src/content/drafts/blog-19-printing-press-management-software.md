In a printing business, almost every order is different. Size, paper type, GSM, colours, quantity, lamination, die-cutting, binding, delivery date. The same customer might order visiting cards on Monday and 5,000 boxes on Wednesday.

That is why printing businesses struggle with generic software. And it is why so much time goes into quotes, job cards and the question every printer hears daily: "Bhaiya, order ready hua?"

Delhi NCR has one of India's biggest printing hubs, from Chawri Bazar to Okhla and Noida, and the problems are the same across most presses.

## Where printing businesses lose time

- **Quotes take too long:** Paper rate, plate cost, printing charges, finishing, wastage, margin. Calculated by hand for every enquiry.
- **Job cards on paper get lost or miss details,** leading to expensive reprints.
- **Proof approvals go back and forth on WhatsApp,** and nobody is sure which version was approved.
- **Job status is unknown** without walking to the press floor. See our guide on [production tracking software for manufacturers](/blog/production-tracking-software-small-manufacturers).
- **Paper stock is checked only when a job is about to start.**
- **Credit customers pile up outstanding** with no clear follow-up.

## What printing press software should do

**A quote calculator built on your rates.** Enter size, paper, GSM, colours, quantity and finishing. The system calculates the price from your own rate cards, including wastage and margin. A quote that took 20 minutes takes two.

**Quote to job card in one click.** When the customer confirms, the quote becomes a job card with every specification. Nothing is retyped.

**Job stages.** Design, proof approval, plate making, printing, finishing, packing and dispatch, or whatever stages your press uses. Each update is one tap.

**Online proof approval.** The customer gets a secure link to view the high-res proof and approve it. The approved version is timestamped and saved with the job.

**Paper and material stock.** Stock of paper reams, boards, inks and consumables, with material reserved against confirmed jobs. Explore our [inventory and billing software](/solutions/inventory-billing-software).

**GST billing and outstanding.** Invoices generated from the job card, payment tracking and automated WhatsApp reminders for credit customers.

**Customer updates.** A WhatsApp notification when the job is ready or dispatched.

## Where online ordering fits

For standard products like visiting cards, flyers, letterheads, packaging boxes and stickers, an online ordering website lets customers choose options, upload their print-ready PDF, pay and place the order themselves. Orders land straight in your job queue.

Custom jobs still come through quotes. Many presses run both: online ordering for standard items and quotes for everything else. Compare [Shopify vs custom e-commerce](/blog/shopify-vs-custom-ecommerce-website-india).

### FAQ

**Can the software calculate printing quotes automatically?**
Yes. It uses your own rate cards for paper, printing, plates and finishing, and calculates the price with wastage and margin.

**Can customers upload their design files online?**
Yes. An online ordering website can accept design uploads, payments and orders for standard products.

**Will it handle both offset and digital printing jobs?**
Yes. Different job types can have their own stages and rate cards.

**Can printing software work with Tally?**
Yes. Invoices and payments can sync with Tally.
