Every evening, your salesmen send a WhatsApp message: "Visited 12 shops. 4 orders. Will follow up tomorrow."

You have no real way to check it. Orders are written on paper and typed up later. Follow-ups depend on memory. And when a good salesman leaves, his customer relationships leave with him.

A field sales app doesn't replace good salesmen. It gives you and them a clear record of what is happening in the market.

## What goes wrong without a system

- Visit reports can't be verified. You have no idea which shops were actually visited.
- Orders are written on paper, then entered late or with mistakes.
- Follow-ups get missed because they live in someone's head.
- No beat discipline. Some areas get visited three times a week, others once a month.
- Customer history is lost when a salesman leaves. Read our guide on [dealer and distributor management software](/blog/dealer-distributor-management-software-india).

## What a good field sales app does

**Beat and route planning.** Each salesman sees which shops or clients to visit today, in a sensible order.

**Visit check-in.** At each visit, a check-in with GPS location and, if useful, a photo of the shop or product shelf display.

**Order taking on the spot.** Product list with prices, schemes and stock. The order reaches the office instantly, not at the end of the day.

**Payment collection entry.** Cash or cheque collected is recorded against the customer, with a receipt sent on WhatsApp.

**Follow-up reminders.** "Call back on Thursday about the new product" becomes a reminder, not a hope.

**Expenses.** Travel and daily allowance claims with photos of fuel or food bills.

**Daily summary.** The app generates the daily report automatically. Salesmen don't have to type it, and you see the same picture every evening.

**Works offline.** Many wholesale markets have weak network. Entries should save on the phone and sync when the network returns.

## Tracking without spying

Location tracking is the part salesmen worry about, and they have a point. Handle it properly:
- Track location only during working hours and only at check-ins or on duty.
- Tell your team in writing what is tracked and why.
- Never track personal time.
- Use the data to plan better routes and support the team, not to embarrass people.

When the app saves salesmen time, for example by writing their daily report for them, adoption is much easier.

### FAQ

**Can salesmen use the app without internet?**
Yes, if it is built with offline mode. Entries are saved on the phone and sync when network returns.

**Is it legal to track salesmen's location?**
Track only during working hours, tell your team clearly in writing, and use the data for work purposes. Speak to your CA or legal advisor about your specific policy.

**Can the app take orders with dealer-specific prices?**
Yes. Each customer's price list and schemes can show automatically when the salesman takes an order.
