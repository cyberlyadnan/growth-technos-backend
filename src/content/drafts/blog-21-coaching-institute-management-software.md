Admission season at a coaching institute is busy. Enquiries come from Instagram ads, Google, walk-ins, calls and referrals. Demo classes are booked. Counsellors follow up. Then the session starts, and the challenge shifts to batches, attendance, tests and fee instalments.

From Mukherjee Nagar and Laxmi Nagar to Noida, most coaching centres we see manage this with Excel, registers and a lot of WhatsApp. It works until it doesn't.

## Where coaching institutes lose admissions and money

- **Enquiries go cold:** A student enquires, nobody calls back for two days, and they join elsewhere.
- **Demo class follow-ups are missed:** The student attended a demo, liked it, and never heard from you again.
- **Fee instalments are tracked manually,** and pending fees pile up quietly.
- **Batch changes and attendance live in different registers.**
- **Parents call the office** for updates on attendance and test results. Learn how this compares with [school management software](/blog/school-management-software-india).

## What coaching institute software should do

**Lead management.** Every enquiry from Meta ads, Google ads, website forms, calls and walk-ins goes into one list. Counsellors get assigned leads with follow-up reminders. Lead stages: new, contacted, demo booked, demo attended, admitted, lost.

**Demo class booking.** Students book demos, get automated WhatsApp reminders, and the counsellor follows up the next day. Explore [WhatsApp automation for businesses](/blog/whatsapp-automation-business-india).

**Admissions and batches.** Admit the student, assign a batch, course and timing, and handle batch transfers cleanly.

**Fee management.** Course fees, discounts, instalment plans, online payments, receipts and automatic reminders before due dates.

**Attendance.** Teachers mark attendance on their phone, and parents get instant alerts for absences.

**Tests and results.** Test marks, ranks within the batch and progress over time, shared with students and parents.

**Online classes.** If you teach online, class links, recordings and attendance in the same system.

**Reports for the owner.** Enquiries by source, conversion by counsellor, fees collected and pending, and batch strength.

## The most valuable part: lead follow-up

If you only fix one thing, fix lead follow-up. Most institutes spend heavily on ads and lose a large share of enquiries to slow or missed follow-ups. Instant WhatsApp replies, fast assignment to counsellors and automated reminders make a massive difference within one admission season.

**Can the software capture leads from Instagram and Facebook ads?**
Yes. Meta lead ads can be connected so enquiries land in the system instantly and get assigned to a counsellor.

Yes. Instalment plans, online payments, automatic receipts and reminders can all be included.

**Can parents see attendance and test results?**
Yes, through WhatsApp alerts, a parent portal or an app, depending on what you need.

Yes. Coaching institutes need stronger lead management, demo class tracking and flexible batches, while schools focus more on classes, sections and report cards.
