## What NCR businesses usually need built

From the businesses we speak to across Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, and Ghaziabad, the needs follow a pattern by industry:

- **Manufacturers in Noida, Sahibabad, Bawana, Narela, Faridabad and Manesar:** Production tracking, raw material stock, dispatch challans and GST billing in one connected system.
- **Traders and distributors in Delhi's wholesale markets (Sadar Bazar, Chandni Chowk, Chawri Bazar):** Dealer orders, dealer-wise pricing, credit limits and payment follow-ups.
- **Travel agencies in Karol Bagh, Connaught Place and South Delhi:** Lead management, quotes, itineraries, and agent tracking.
- **Schools and coaching institutes in Laxmi Nagar, Kalu Sarai and Mukherjee Nagar:** Admissions, fee collection, attendance and parent updates.
- **Real estate firms in Noida Extension, Gurugram and Ghaziabad:** Portal leads from 99acres and MagicBricks, site visits, unit inventory and channel partners.
- **Clinics and diagnostic centres:** Appointment booking, WhatsApp reminders and patient follow-ups.

Most of these businesses don't need a giant enterprise ERP. They need one focused system that fixes the two or three problems costing them the most time and money.

## Does your software company need to be local?

Honestly, less than you might think. Most software work happens remotely now: video calls, screen shares, a shared WhatsApp group and a project tracker.

However, meeting in person still helps at two critical moments:

1. **At the start:** Walking through your office, warehouse, or factory floor shows a developer operational details that no remote call ever will.
2. **At launch:** Training your ground staff and office team face-to-face makes user adoption much smoother.

So ask any company you shortlist: will you visit us once at the start for a workflow audit, and once at launch for staff training?

## How to shortlist in five steps

1. **Ask for live work similar to yours:** Insist on a real, working system you can click through—not just static Figma design mockups.
2. **Meet the person who will build it:** Talk to the actual lead engineer, not just a commission-driven salesperson.
3. **Get a detailed scope document:** Ensure every screen, role, and workflow is written down before you make any payment. Review our [how to choose a software development company](/blog/how-to-choose-software-development-company-india) guide for details.
4. **Ask when you will see a working version:** A working demo in 10 to 14 days is reasonable. Three months without seeing a screen is a major warning sign.
5. **Confirm ownership:** Complete source code, cloud hosting, and domain accounts must be registered in your name. Use our [software requirements template](/blog/software-requirements-document-template) to prepare your brief.
